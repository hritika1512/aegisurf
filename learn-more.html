How to Earn Your Certificate

Watch the overview video below to learn how to complete the cybersecurity modules, pass the end-of-course quiz, and qualify for your certificate.

Complete the Course

Work through each online safety lesson—covering phishing, strong passwords, safe browsing, and privacy—at your own pace.

02

Pass the Quiz

Take the final quiz once all modules are finished. You need to score at least 80% to pass (retakes are available).

03

Claim Your Certificate

Be signed in with your name/account details and pass the quiz with 80% or higher to unlock and download your completion certificate.
